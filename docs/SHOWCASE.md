## Community Themes

- **Pitch Black OLED (Default):** Pure `#07080b` black background with dynamic ambient glow matching the focused app.
- **Northern Aurora (`themes/aurora.json`):** Deep oceanic cyan gradient with luminous frosted buttons.
- **Midnight Glass (`themes/midnight.json`):** Deep navy blue translucency with high-contrast icons.
- **Monochrome Quiet (`themes/minimal.json`):** High contrast, zero glow, minimal visual distraction.
